# Customer Churn Prediction & Retention Analytics

## Project Overview

Customer retention is one of the most important business challenges for subscription-based companies. Acquiring a new customer is significantly more expensive than retaining an existing one. Therefore, identifying customers who are likely to churn enables businesses to proactively take retention actions and reduce revenue loss.

This project develops an end-to-end machine learning solution to predict customer churn using demographic, service usage, billing, and customer account information. The project also provides business insights through SQL analysis and an executive Power BI dashboard.

---

## Business Problem

The telecom company experiences customer attrition every month, resulting in reduced recurring revenue and increased customer acquisition costs. The business requires a predictive solution to identify customers at high risk of churn and understand the key factors influencing customer departure.

---

## Business Objectives

- Predict customer churn accurately.
- Identify the major drivers of churn.
- Segment high-risk customers.
- Recommend customer retention strategies.
- Build an executive dashboard for decision-makers.

---

## Success Criteria

### Business Success

- Reduce customer churn.
- Improve customer retention.
- Increase Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV).
- Support targeted marketing campaigns.

### Technical Success

- Build a reliable binary classification model.
- Achieve strong Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Deploy the solution using Streamlit.
